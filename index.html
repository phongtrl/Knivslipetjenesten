About Me

I'm Phong — better known online as NAIFU ナイフ. Ever since I was a kid I'd grab any rock I could find to sharpen my tools. It never really worked — until 2019, when my first cheap whetstone made everything click.

By 2020 I had turned that obsession into a real business. Many know me as one of the owners of Tomatosharp, sharpening for professional chefs and home cooks and selling knives and kitchen tools from 2020 to 2025.

That journey came to an end in 2025, when I chose to take the next step and focus on my own career — first in engineering, with a Master's and Bachelor's in Geography, Geomatics & Spatial Planning from NTNU Trondheim, and then on social media as “NAIFU”, sharing the quiet focus of the craft with my community on TikTok.

"Every knife tells a story. My job is to listen to it through the steel."

What I Don't Offer

To keep quality high, some jobs are outside my service.

  • Major damage and blade reconstruction
  • Serrated knives
  • Hunting knives
  • Thinning of knives that have become too thick from repeated sharpening

If I discover elements I don't handle, I may choose to leave certain knives unsharpened.
